Security & Control

Your infrastructure. Your control.

ConvOps has access to your AWS account. Here's exactly what that means — no vague claims.

Read-only by default

ConvOps reads CloudWatch metrics, logs, and resource state. No write access until you explicitly enable it. You choose which services can be acted on.

Manual approval required

No action executes automatically. Ever. You reply YES to confirm, NO to cancel. Confirmation is phone-bound and expires after 5 minutes.

Full audit logs

Every investigation and action logged with timestamp, resource ARN, who approved it, and outcome. 90-day retention. Export anytime.

Scoped IAM permissions

One CloudFormation template. Minimum permissions needed — nothing more. Review or delete the IAM role anytime from your AWS console.

arn:aws:iam::YOUR_ACCOUNT:role/ConvOpsReadOnlyRole
15-min STS sessions

Tokens expire after 15 minutes. No long-lived credentials stored outside your AWS account.

Revoke instantly

Delete the IAM role from your AWS account. ConvOps loses all access immediately — no support ticket needed.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this safe to connect to my AWS account?
ConvOps deploys a single CloudFormation template that creates: an IAM role with AWS-managed ReadOnlyAccess policy (read-only across all services), an SNS topic to receive CloudWatch alarm notifications, and an EventBridge rule to route alarm state changes. It cannot modify, delete, or create any AWS resource in the free plan. The IAM role is protected by a unique ExternalId and has a 1-hour max session duration. Action execution (Pro) requires explicit human approval for every action — ConvOps never runs anything autonomously. You can revoke access instantly by deleting the IAM role.
How long does the AI investigation take?
Under 60 seconds from when CloudWatch fires the alarm to when you receive the RCA on WhatsApp or Slack.
What AWS services are supported?
ConvOps works with any service that sends metrics to CloudWatch — ECS, Lambda, EC2, RDS, ALB, and more. If CloudWatch can alarm on it, ConvOps can investigate it.
What happens when I hit the 25 incident limit on the free plan?
You stop receiving AI investigations for the rest of that month. Your CloudWatch alarms still fire normally — ConvOps just won't enrich them until the next billing cycle. You'll get a warning message when you're approaching the limit.
What if ConvOps has downtime during an incident?
Your CloudWatch alarms fire independently of ConvOps. If ConvOps is unavailable, you receive a standard CloudWatch notification via SNS as a fallback. Your monitoring doesn't depend on ConvOps being up.
Is this GDPR compliant? Where is my data stored?
ConvOps processes CloudWatch log data in the same AWS region as your account. Log content is not stored beyond the investigation window. Need a GDPR Data Processing Agreement or other compliance paperwork — talk to the founder.
Do I need to configure my CloudWatch alarms differently?
No. ConvOps connects to your existing CloudWatch alarms via SNS. No changes to alarm thresholds, namespaces, or configurations required.
Can I disconnect ConvOps at any time?
Yes. Delete the IAM role from your AWS console and ConvOps immediately loses all access. No support ticket, no waiting period.
